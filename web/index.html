Claim boundary

Zero differences is a scoped result.

The 672,321 comparisons cover the declared JSON-compatible dense subset across 32 parse and 24 stringify templates. The 1045/1045 figure is the frozen JavaScript baseline—not tests executed directly against Go. Tagged JavaScript-only values, callbacks, cycles, and an exhaustive one-to-one mapping of all upstream test blocks are deferred.
